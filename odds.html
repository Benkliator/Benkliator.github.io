Sky Crate odds

Last updated: 24 November 2026

The Sky Crate holds cosmetic skins for the aircraft you fly, and a few aircraft. It is opened only with Wings, the currency you earn by playing (and, on phones, by ads you choose to watch). Wings are never sold, so a crate opening can't be bought with money.

How an opening is drawn

Each opening first picks a tier:

TierChance, of a full crate
Aircraft1%
Legendary4%
Epic10%
Rare35%
Common50%

A tier that's used up shares its chance with the others in proportion. Then the opening gives any item of that tier still in your crate, each equally likely.

  • No duplicates. You never get an item you already own: every opening is something new, until the crate is empty.
  • An aircraft is guaranteed in time. After 80 openings in a row without an aircraft, the next opening gives one, while there is an aircraft left in your crate.
  • The crate's page in the game shows your odds as they are now, per tier and per item, before every opening.

Getting an item without the crate

Any skin or aircraft in the crate can also be had directly, with no chance involved: on phones with flight coins, at a fixed price by rarity; on PC with Wings, at a fixed price. An item bought this way leaves your crate.
