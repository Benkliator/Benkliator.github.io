Outflown: King of the Sky

Arcade jet dogfights in pixel art, for phones and PC.

Duel friends and computer pilots over hand-built stages: the open ocean, a frozen fjord, a canyon at sunset, a city at night. Survive waves of drones and gunships with up to four pilots, race through the gates of Sky Race, take on the boss in the storm, and win the tournament to be crowned King of the Sky.

Fly on touch, a controller or keys. On PC, up to four can play on one screen.

Coming soon to PC (Steam), iPhone, iPad and Android.
