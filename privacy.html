Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what data the game Outflown: King of the Sky ("the game") and the services it uses collect, why, for how long, and the choices you have. The game is made by an independent developer based in Sweden ("we").

In short

  • The PC version (Steam) has no account with us and sends us nothing. Your progress stays on your computer.
  • The phone versions (iPhone, iPad, Android) are played connected to our game server. It keeps your account, your progress and what you own, so they can't be lost or faked.
  • The phone versions show ads from Google AdMob. The PC version shows none.
  • We don't sell your data. You can delete your account in the game at any time.

The PC version (Steam)

Your progress, settings, unlocks and high scores are saved on your own computer. The PC version has no player account with us, doesn't talk to our server and shows no ads. It uses no analytics or crash-reporting service.

Playing with others on PC goes through Steam (friends, invites and lobbies, under Valve's Privacy Policy) or over your own network. In a match hosted by a player, your IP address is visible to that player's computer, and your pilot name and in-match actions are shared with the others in the match. Purchases of the game are handled by Steam.

The phone versions: what our game server keeps

On iPhone, iPad and Android the game signs you in with Apple's Game Center or Google Play Games and plays connected to our game server. The server keeps:

  • Your account: a player id we create, your Game Center or Google Play Games player id, your pilot name and tag, and when the account was created and last used. We don't get your real name, email address or password from Apple or Google.
  • How you play: matches and their results, ratings and ladder tiers, Survival runs and scores, tournament brackets, achievements, daily tasks and the aircraft, stages and modes you have opened.
  • Your wallet: Wings and flight coins, the skins, aircraft, icons and titles you own, a record of every change to them, your purchases (the store's transaction ids and, for Premium, whether the subscription is running and until when) and the rewarded ads that paid you something (the ad unit and AdMob's transaction id).
  • Your cloud save: the parts of your profile that follow you to another phone, such as the skins you have equipped and your local best times.
  • Friends and safety: your friends and friend requests, pilots you blocked, reports you made or that were made about you, and any sanction on the account.
  • Device checks: whether your device passed Apple's DeviceCheck or Google's Play Integrity check, which tell us the game runs on a real, unmodified device.
  • Notifications, if you allow them: your phone's notification token is kept with your account to send you friend requests and invites. It's deleted with the account, or when you turn notifications off. The reminder that the daily tasks have reset is made on your phone itself.
  • Recordings of online matches: for each match the server keeps its input log (every pilot's controls and the match's setup, from which the match can be played again) and its result. They are used to check results, review reports and balance the game, are seen only by the game's staff, and are deleted after 30 days.
  • Statistics and crash reports, if you leave them on: while Options → ONLINE → Share stats is on, the game sends anonymous daily counters (for example, which modes are played). After a crash it asks whether to send the log; it's sent only if you say yes. Turn Share stats off and neither is sent.

Server logs and where players are

The game services' logs are kept for 30 days and hold your IP address only in a coarse form (its network and a short keyed hash, never the whole address). The server's system logs (the web server and remote access) hold whole addresses for 7 days. A security record of sign-ins, purchases, sanctions and staff actions is kept for 400 days.

When you sign in, your IP address is looked up in an offline country table and then dropped. The server counts players per day and country, and how fast their connection to each server region is. No address, city or position is stored for any player, and a country with fewer than five players is counted only as "other". IP geolocation by DB-IP, CC BY 4.0.

Why we use this data

  • To run the game for you (our agreement with you): your account, progress, wallet, purchases, cloud save, friends and online matches.
  • To keep play fair and the service secure (our legitimate interest): device checks, match recordings, reports, sanctions, logs and the security record.
  • To improve the game (our legitimate interest, and your choice): the anonymous counters, the country counts and the crash logs you agree to send.
  • With your consent: notifications and personalised ads (below).

Advertising (phone versions only)

The phone versions show ads from Google AdMob: ads you choose to watch for a reward, and occasional ads between matches. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA) and other device information;
  • your approximate location, based on your IP address;
  • how you interact with ads (for example, views and taps).

This data is collected and processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services. When an ad pays you a reward, Google tells our server so, and the server records it with your account.

Your choices:

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in US states with privacy laws, the game asks for your consent (or offers an opt-out) before personalised ads are shown, through Google's consent form.
  • On iPhone and iPad, Apple asks whether the game may track you. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalised.
  • You can review or change your choice at any time in the game under Options → ADS.
  • You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your phone's settings.
  • A Premium subscription removes the ads between matches while it runs.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store, Google Play or Steam. We never see your payment details. On phones, the store gives the game a receipt, which our server checks with the store and records with your account, so that what you bought is yours on any phone you sign in on.

Who else handles your data

  • netcup GmbH (Germany): hosts our game server, in a data centre in the European Union.
  • Backblaze: stores encrypted backups of the server's database. A backup is kept for 30 days.
  • Apple and Google: sign-in (Game Center, Google Play Games), purchases, device checks and, if you allow them, the delivery of notifications.
  • Google AdMob: ads, as described above.
  • Valve (PC): Steam's own services.

Some of these companies may process data outside the EU/EEA, under their own safeguards and policies. We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, progress, wallet and friends: until you delete the account.
  • The details of individual matches: about 13 months. Match recordings: 30 days.
  • Logs: 30 days (whole IP addresses: 7 days). The security record: 400 days.
  • Backups: 30 days after they were made.

Deleting your account

On a phone: Options → ONLINE → Delete account. It takes effect at once and removes your account and what belongs to it from the game server; backups age out within 30 days. You can also ask by email (below). If the account is under a sanction, a keyed hash of the sign-in stays until the sanction ends, so that deleting an account isn't a way round a ban; it holds nothing else about you.

On PC, uninstalling the game and deleting its save folder removes everything; we hold nothing.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you have the right to see, correct, export or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw a consent you gave. Write to us at the address below; we answer within a month. Data collected by Google, Apple or Valve is handled by them: use their tools or contact them directly. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in Sweden that is the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
