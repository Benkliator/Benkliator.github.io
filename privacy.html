Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains what data the game King of the Sky ("the game") and the services it uses collect, and the choices you have. The game is made by an independent developer based in Sweden.

What the game itself keeps

Your progress, settings, unlocks, high scores and purchases are saved on your own device. The game does not have player accounts and does not send this data to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

The game does not use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Advertising (Android and iOS)

The phone versions show ads from Google AdMob: ads you choose to watch for a reward, and occasional ads between matches. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA) and other device information;
  • your approximate location, based on your IP address;
  • how you interact with ads (for example, views and taps).

This data is collected and processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices:

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in US states with privacy laws, the game asks for your consent (or offers an opt-out) before personalised ads are shown, through Google's consent form.
  • On iOS, Apple asks whether the game may track you. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalised.
  • You can review or change your choice at any time in the game under Options → ADS.
  • You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your phone's settings.

The PC version shows no ads.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play, the Apple App Store or Steam. We never see your payment details. The store tells the game that a purchase went through, and the game records it on your device.

Online play

When you play online, the game connects your device directly to the other players in the match. To do this, your IP address is visible to them, and the game shares your pilot name and in-match actions with them. On PC, online play can also go through Steam, under Valve's Privacy Policy. We do not run servers that store this data.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data and to object to its use. Data collected by Google or Valve is handled by them; you can use their tools or contact them directly. For anything else, contact us below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/Benkliator/Benkliator.github.io/issues.
